# Contributing

Contributions to **kushal** are welcome. Accepted forms:

- referrals
- introductions
- interesting problems
- interesting problems disguised as boring ones (accepted with gratitude)

## process

1. open `contact.env` — the `EMAIL=` line is not redacted
2. send the thing
3. mention calendars for the <2h response SLA (`RESPONSE_TIME_IF_YOU_SAY_CALENDAR`)

## code of conduct

Be the reviewer you'd want at 5pm on a Friday. Snacks encouraged
but not required (see `LICENSE`).

## tests

`npm test` runs `tests/hireable.test.ts`. It is 6/6 green and
will remain so. The bar is kept intentionally low and is tripped
over daily anyway.
